Get Unlimited back on a new phone

  1. Sign in to the same Google Play or App Store account you bought with.
  2. In APrompter, open the menu at the top right of the home screen (⋮ on Android, ⋯ on iPhone) and tap Upgrade. If Upgrade isn't there, Unlimited is already active.
  3. Tap Restore purchases.

A purchase made on Google Play can't be moved to an iPhone, and the other way round.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not inside the app.

  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → APrompter.
  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → APrompter.

To be sure you aren't charged again, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You keep Unlimited until then. Refunds are handled by Google or Apple.

Back up your scripts

Your scripts live on your phone. To keep a copy, open the menu at the top right of the home screen (⋮ / ⋯) and tap Back up all scripts, then save the file to Drive, Files or email. Restore from a backup in the same menu brings them back.

Bring in a script from another app

Share the note to APrompter from Google Keep, Samsung Notes or Apple Notes. From Notion or Google Docs, copy the text and tap New script → Paste as new script (in Google Docs you can also use Share & export → Send a copy → Word). You can also open .txt, .md, .docx, .rtf, .odt, .html, Evernote and subtitle files with Import files.

Float doesn't appear (Android)

Float needs permission to display over other apps. Tap Float again and Android opens the setting: turn on Display over other apps (on Samsung: Appear on top) for APrompter, go back and tap Float. Android Go and other low-memory phones can't float apps, so there you film with Record.

Your data

APrompter has no account and never sends your scripts or videos anywhere. To ask for a copy of your purchase records, or to have them corrected or deleted, email nightowlyyyy@gmail.com with the subject "My APrompter data", the store you bought in and the order number from the store's receipt. For deletion, cancel any subscription and uninstall APrompter first. The privacy policy explains the details.
