Features

What's in the course

A writing check that tells you the rule

Write the email you really have to send. Dutchy underlines the mistake in your own sentence and tells you which rule it breaks.

Ik ga naar de huisarts omdat ik heb pijn in mijn rug.

After “omdat” the finite verb goes to the end of the clause.

  • It checks sentence structure: the verb in second place, the verb at the end of a subordinate clause, agreement, de/het and adjective endings. It doesn't comment on style.
  • The check runs on your phone, so your text stays there
  • When it isn't sure, it says nothing. It's tested against all the correct Dutch in the course, and it doesn't flag any of it.
  • There's a writing desk with templates for the landlord, the gemeente, a sick note, a job application and the neighbours

Pronunciation, sound by sound

Say a sentence and see which sound came out wrong: your g as a k, your ui as an ou. Dutchy keeps track of every sound and makes drills from course words that contain your weakest ones.

  • You get a score per sound, like “your ui is at 27%, your g at 45%”
  • Every wrong sound is marked in your own sentence, with a play button to hear it
  • What it can't do: it hears a sound that comes out as a different Dutch sound, but it doesn't judge your accent, and it won't make up a mistake.

Practice conversations

Six role-plays: the bakery, the huisarts, the gemeente, a landlord, a job interview and the neighbours. Every line is written out in advance, so the other person says what Dutch people really say, including the questions that tend to catch learners out.

  • What you mean counts for more than perfect grammar
  • If you aren't understood, the other person asks again
  • Afterwards you get a summary: what you managed to do, grammar notes on what you typed, and the sounds in what you said

Exam practice

Turn on exam mode when you have an exam date. Until then you won't see it.

  • Inburgering A2 and B1 and Staatsexamen NT2 Programma I and II
  • Mock exams with the same sections and timing as the real exam, a score per section, and a note on which section you're not ready for yet
  • KNM, the compulsory part about Dutch society: 54 questions written to the exam requirements that apply since 1 July 2025, each with an explanation of the right answer

From A1 to C2

The units sit on one path, with a marker on the one you're doing. Spaced repetition brings words back just before you'd forget them, and anything you get wrong comes back sooner. In each lesson you recognise, use, hear and say the same word.

  • A 16-question placement test, or just pick your level
  • Immersion mode: instructions only in Dutch, and a story in eight chapters that goes from A1 to B1
  • Daily quests. You can also set a limit of five mistakes a day, but it's off unless you turn it on

Works offline

The whole course is on your phone, so it works on a train with no signal. You don't sign in, and there's no server.

Take your notes with you

Export your word bank and notes as Markdown, plain text, CSV, Anki or JSON, or share them to Notes, Keep, Obsidian, Notion or WhatsApp.

Accessibility

Labels for screen readers, English translations marked as English, colours checked for colour-blind learners, and a tick or a cross next to every answer.

A phrasebook

Sentences for emergencies, shops, transport, officials and work. It's always free, like the dictionary and the grammar reference.

About the levels

What the levels mean

A level describes the material. It doesn't promise anything about you. What each level does follow is what the matching exam tests: the inburgeringsexamen at A2 and B1, and Staatsexamen NT2 Programma I and II at B1 and B2. There's no Dutch state exam above B2, so C1 and C2 follow the CEFR only, and the app tells you that.

Privacy

Your progress stays on your phone

Dutchy has no server of its own, and nothing about your learning leaves your phone. The only network traffic the app causes is the purchase check. There's one thing to know: speech recognition is done by your phone's own speech service, and depending on your phone, that may send audio to Google or Apple. Read the privacy statement.

  • No account

    There's nothing to sign up for. A subscription is tied to your Google Play or App Store account.

  • No ads

    Not on the free level, and not on the paid one.

  • No tracking

    There are no analytics tools. If you report a problem, you see the report and send it yourself.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. If you subscribe, it belongs to your Google Play or App Store account, and Aankopen herstellen (Restore purchases) brings it back on a new phone.

Is it only for the exam?

No. It's a full course from A1 to C2. Exam mode is one part of it, and you turn it on when you have a date.

Will finishing a level make me that level?

No course can promise that, and this one doesn't. Each level follows the material the matching exam covers; only an official exam says what your level is.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole course is in the app. Speech recognition may need a connection on some phones. Everything else works without one.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. It's planned for after the Android version. Email me if you'd like to hear when it's out.

Where is my progress stored?

On your phone, inside the app, and nowhere else. Because nobody else has a copy, the app keeps a snapshot of each day and lets you export a backup.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In your store account. On Android: Google Play, then Profile, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
