Features

A course that teaches what apps usually skip

Writing that names the rule

Write the email you actually have to send. Dutchy marks the mistake in your own sentence and tells you which rule you broke, not just that it was wrong.

Ik ga naar de dokter omdat ik heb pijn in mijn rug.

After “omdat” the finite verb moves to the end of the clause.

  • Structure, not style: verb second, verb last in subordinate clauses, agreement, de/het, adjective endings
  • Runs on your phone, so your text never leaves it
  • Stays silent rather than guess: it is tested to report nothing on any of the correct Dutch the course ships
  • The writing desk: a blank page with templates for the landlord, the gemeente, a sick note, a job application and the neighbours

Pronunciation, sound by sound

Say a sentence and see which sound came out wrong: your g as a k, your ui as an ou. Dutchy keeps a profile per sound and builds drills from course words that contain your weakest ones.

  • “Your ui is at 27%, your g at 45%” instead of “your pronunciation needs work”
  • Every wrong sound is marked in your own sentence, with a play button to hear it
  • Honest limits: it catches sounds that arrive as a different Dutch sound. It doesn't judge your accent and never invents a fault.

Conversations to fumble in

Six role-plays: the bakery, the huisarts, the gemeente, a landlord, a job interview and the neighbours. They are scripted, not generated, so every line the other person says is Dutch somebody really says, including the questions that catch learners out.

  • Say what you mean: it is forgiving about form and strict about content
  • If you aren't understood, the other person asks again, as a person would
  • A debrief covers what you got done, the grammar of what you typed and the sounds of what you said

Exam practice, when you need it

Switch exam mode on when a date lands in your diary. Until then it stays out of the way.

  • Inburgering A2 and B1 and Staatsexamen NT2 Programma I and II
  • Mock exams with the sections and timing of the real thing, a score per section, and a note on which section you won't pass yet
  • KNM: the compulsory Dutch-society paper, with 54 questions written to the exam requirements in force since 1 July 2025, and an explanation of why each answer is right

One path from A1 to C2

A path of units with a marker on the one you are on. Spaced repetition brings back what you are about to forget, and anything you miss returns sooner. Every lesson mixes recognising, producing, hearing and saying the same word.

  • A 16-question placement test, or just pick your level
  • Immersion mode: Dutch-only instructions and an eight-chapter story that climbs from A1 to B1
  • Daily quests. A limit of five mistakes a day is optional and off by default

Honest labels

What the levels mean

A level label describes the material, not a promise about you. What each level does claim is that it follows what the relevant exam examines: the inburgeringsexamen at A2 and B1, and Staatsexamen NT2 Programma I and II at B1 and B2. Above B2 there is no Dutch state exam, so C1 and C2 follow the CEFR only, and the app says so.

Privacy

FAQ

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. If you subscribe, it belongs to your Google Play or App Store account, and Aankopen herstellen (Restore purchases) brings it back on a new phone.

Is it only for the exam?

No. It is a full course from A1 to C2, and exam mode is a part you switch on when you have a date. People often find Dutchy by searching for the exam, then keep going because the next level is right there.

Will finishing a level make me that level?

No course can promise that, and this one doesn't. Each level follows the material the matching exam covers; only an official exam says what your level is.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole course is in the app. Speech recognition may need a connection on some phones, and everything else works without one.

Is there an iPhone version?

It is planned. Android testing comes first. Email to be told when it is ready.

Where is my progress stored?

On your phone, inside the app, and nowhere else. The app keeps a daily snapshot and lets you export a backup, because nobody else holds a copy that could restore it for you.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In your store account. On Android: Google Play, then Profile, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
