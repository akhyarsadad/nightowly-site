Get Dutchy Volledig back on a new phone

  1. Sign in to the same Google Play or App Store account you subscribed with.
  2. In Dutchy, open Instellingen (Settings), find Abonnement and open the subscription screen.
  3. Tap Aankopen herstellen (Restore purchases).

A subscription bought on Google Play can't be moved to an iPhone, and the other way round.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not inside the app.

  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Dutchy.
  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Dutchy.

To be sure you aren't charged again, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You keep Dutchy Volledig until then. Refunds are handled by Google or Apple.

Keep your progress, or move it to a new phone

Your progress lives on your phone and nowhere else, so nobody can restore it for you. Dutchy keeps a snapshot of each day and can export a backup file. In Instellingen, open Back-up en herstel to export a backup or go back to an earlier day. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, erases your progress, so export first if you want to keep it.

A speaking exercise doesn't work

  • No microphone: allow Dutchy to use the microphone (and, on iPhone, speech recognition) in your phone's settings.
  • Nothing is recognised: try a quieter place. Some phones need an internet connection for speech recognition, because the recognition is done by your phone's own speech service.
  • No Dutch voice to listen to: install one. On Android: Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech.
  • You can skip speaking exercises. The rest of the course works without them.

Your data

Dutchy has no account and never sends your progress or what you write anywhere. To ask for a copy of your purchase records, or to have them corrected or deleted, email nightowlyyyy@gmail.com with the subject "My Dutchy data", the store you bought in and the order number from the store's receipt. For deletion, cancel any subscription and uninstall Dutchy first. The privacy statement explains the details.

Report a bug or a mistake in the course

Email is best. Say which unit or screen it was on, and what you expected. I especially want to hear about answers or explanations that are wrong.
