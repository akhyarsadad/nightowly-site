About

Who makes these

Just me. I write the code, design the screens, write the text in the apps and on this site, and answer the support emails.

Each app is for one kind of person with one thing to get done. You don't need an account for any of them, there are no ads, and they don't track what you do.

What you make in them, like a script, your notes or your progress, is stored on your phone. The apps only go online to check whether you've bought something. The one exception is Dutchy's speaking exercises: they use your phone's own speech recognition, and depending on your phone, that can go online.

Each app has its own privacy policy and terms. They're short, so it's worth reading them rather than taking my word for it.
